# UsageWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-11

UsageWatch is a companion app that helps you view Claude Code usage snapshots from your own Mac on your iPhone, iPad, widgets, and Apple Watch.

## Data Usage

In the default local setup, UsageWatch does not collect personal data for the developer. Usage snapshots are fetched from the UsageWatch CLI running on your Mac and stored locally on your devices so the app, widgets, and watch app can display them.

Usage snapshots may include:

- Claude Code usage percentages and reset times.
- Session cost or token metrics when available.
- Your Mac device name as reported by the CLI.
- Refresh timestamps.

## Camera

UsageWatch uses the camera only to scan the pairing QR code printed by the Mac CLI. Camera frames are not stored or sent to the developer.

## Local Network

UsageWatch uses local network access to connect to the UsageWatch CLI on your Mac over the same Wi-Fi network.

## Developer Collection

The default local Mac-to-iPhone/iPad flow does not send usage snapshots to the developer. If a hosted relay is offered later, this policy must be updated before that relay is used in production.

## Third Parties

UsageWatch displays data produced by Claude Code. UsageWatch is not affiliated with Anthropic, Claude, Apple, or OpenAI.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/sufferfml/Usagewatch/issues.
